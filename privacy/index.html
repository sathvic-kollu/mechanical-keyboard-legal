Mechanical Keyboard Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026 for RGB Mechanical Keyboard Sounds (Android package app.mechanicalkeyboard.keysounds), published by Zoopet. Privacy enquiries: contact@zoopet.in. The developer responsible for the released app is identified in its store listing; this policy applies specifically to Mechanical Keyboard, not to other apps using the same support inbox.

1. What the app does

Mechanical Keyboard provides an Android software keyboard and a companion app for creating and managing visual scenes, sound and comfort settings. Core typing does not require an app account. The app is independent of other keyboard publishers.

2. Keyboard access and typed text

An enabled keyboard receives text-editing information from the current editor so it can enter characters, compose supported scripts, offer local candidates, replace selections and move the cursor. Android displays a warning about this access. Enable only keyboards you trust. Our keyboard code does not send typed text, editor contents or the identity of the app you are typing in to our receipt service, advertising services or analytics. We do not automatically learn and permanently save every word you type.

2.1 Private input

Password fields and the keyboard's Incognito setting suppress word suggestions, saved-clipboard tools and key sound. Basic Korean and Vietnamese composition remains local; Chinese candidate selection is unavailable in private fields. These controls do not make the app you are typing into private, erase that app's records or protect against a compromised device.

3. Data stored on your device

We store keyboard preferences, the selected design, drafts, saved scenes and favourites, manually added personal words, explicitly saved clipboard entries, creative-day progress, reminder preferences, an app-generated installation identifier and purchase-verification state. Settings, personal words and saved clipboard content use authenticated encrypted storage protected by Android Keystore. This is not a cloud backup. The installation identifier is generated for this installation, not derived from IMEI, Android ID or a phone number.

3.1 Clipboard

Clipboard history is not automatically collected. An entry is saved only when you choose Save clipboard. Up to 20 entries may be kept. Unpinned entries become unavailable after one hour and are physically pruned on a subsequent state update; pinned entries remain until unpinned, deleted or local data is cleared. Do not pin passwords or other secrets. Copy, Cut and Paste also interact with Android's clipboard, which is separate from our saved list.

3.2 Photos

You choose a background through Android's system photo picker. We read the chosen image and make a bounded, resized copy in the app's private files. Photos are not uploaded by our photo feature, included in scene codes or protected by the settings-file encryption. Original gallery images are not modified or deleted. Remove photo removes the selected local background. We do not request access to your entire gallery for this feature.

3.3 Scene sharing

A scene code contains the scene name you entered, visual parameters and the sound choice. It does not include photos, personal-dictionary words, clipboard entries or purchase entitlements. The name is not secret: share only names you are willing to disclose. When you use the Android share sheet, the chosen receiving app handles the shared code or store link under its own terms. Copies already sent to other people cannot be recalled by clearing local data.

3.4 Local installation attribution

The Google Play build can read a bounded set of installation-referrer campaign fields and timestamps. Accepted campaign labels are sanitised and stored locally to describe the installation source. They are not sent to our servers or used as cross-app identifiers. The Galaxy build does not contain this Play-specific capture implementation. Clearing local data removes this record.

4. Companion-app advertising

When advertising is configured, the companion app can show banner, native, interstitial and return-to-app ads. Our placements are not in the keyboard or over another app's editor. Google AdMob and its mediation partners may process IP addresses, advertising and other device identifiers, device/app information, ad interactions, diagnostics and approximate location derived from network information, subject to the applicable settings and consent requirements. Choosing non-personalised or limited ads does not necessarily stop all technical data processing.

4.1 Advertising partners

The app uses Google AdMob, with Unity Ads as its only mediation partner. Whether a partner serves an ad depends on consent and availability. Read their policies: Google and Unity. This list does not mean every partner receives data on every visit.

4.2 Your ad choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform for required advertising consent and privacy choices. Open Settings → Ad privacy choices when available to revisit those choices. You can also manage Android's advertising settings. A verified Remove Ads purchase prevents our companion-ad placements from loading or showing while ownership is valid. It does not erase previously processed service data. Typed text is not used by our code as advertising context.

5. Diagnostics and remote configuration

This version of the app does not include Firebase, Crashlytics, analytics SDKs or remote configuration, and it does not use a server-push notification service. If a future version adds optional diagnostics, this policy and the store's data-safety information will be updated before that version is released.

6. Purchases

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase through the store that distributes your build. The Play build uses Google Play Billing; the Galaxy build uses Samsung In-App Purchase. The store processes payment and account details; the app never receives your card number. Prices are shown by the store for your country.

The app does not send purchase receipts to our own servers. To decide whether ads should show, it asks the store on your device which products your store account owns, and keeps a small local record of that result. It re-checks with the store when the app starts or returns to the foreground, so refunds or revocations remove the benefit. Purchases belong to your store account and can be restored by reinstalling the app on a device signed in to the same account. Core typing and customisation never require a purchase.

7. Reminders and system services

Optional creative reminders are scheduled on the device, have frequency limits and pause after repeated ignored alerts. Notification permission is requested only when you choose the feature after the first calendar day. You may turn reminders off in the app or Android settings. There is no remote push transport. Android and the distributing store may separately process information for app updates, in-app reviews and their platform services.

8. Support messages

Contact opens your email app with app version, Android version and device model for you to review. Nothing is emailed automatically. The message, your address and any attachments you choose to send are handled by your mail provider and our support mailbox. Do not send passwords, private typing samples or full payment tokens. Support messages are used to address the request, maintain necessary support records and meet applicable legal obligations; their deletion can be requested at the contact address.

9. Retention and deletion

Local settings and saved designs remain until you change, delete or clear them, or uninstall the app. Clipboard expiry is described above. Android backup is disabled in the supplied configuration; verify the installed build and device behaviour. Uninstalling does not delete store purchase records, previously shared scene codes, support emails or data already held by service providers. Provider data follows the relevant service settings and retention terms rather than one universal period.

Use Settings → Clear my data and confirm to remove local preferences, designs, imported photos, personal words, saved clipboard entries, progress and cached purchase state. It also resets optional analytics and deletes unsent crash reports. Purchases can be restored separately. There is no Mechanical Keyboard account to delete. See the data-deletion page for instructions and requests concerning service-held data.

10. Sharing, service providers and transfers

We do not sell your typed text or send it to ad partners. That narrow statement does not claim that all advertising processing is exempt from legal definitions of sharing. Advertising, diagnostics, stores, hosting and email providers process their respective data for service delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, support and legal purposes. They may process information in other countries under applicable safeguards. The publisher must use appropriate provider agreements and consent choices for the markets served.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy, withdraw consent or complain to a privacy authority. Contact contact@zoopet.in and name Mechanical Keyboard and the relevant service or request. We may need proportionate information to verify a request; do not send government identification unless specifically necessary and requested securely. Local-only information cannot be recovered from a server that never received it.

12. Children and audience

Mechanical Keyboard is a general keyboard customisation utility, not a child-directed service. The publisher must set truthful store age/content declarations and age-appropriate advertising configuration before release. A store content rating is not consent to collect children's data. Parents or guardians with a concern should contact the privacy inbox; stop optional collection while a concern is assessed. The app does not ask for a date of birth.

13. Security

We use app-private storage, authenticated encryption for structured settings, HTTPS for first-party verification and signed ownership records. These measures reduce risks but cannot guarantee security on every device or recover lost local files. Protect your device and avoid saving secrets in scene names, personal words or pinned clipboard entries.

14. Changes and contact

The published version and date of this policy should be updated when released data practices change. Material new processing requires the appropriate notice and, where required, consent before it starts. For questions or deletion requests, email contact@zoopet.in with the app name. Legal-policy and Play contacts use that inbox; the separate Galaxy Seller Portal contact field does not change this privacy address.
