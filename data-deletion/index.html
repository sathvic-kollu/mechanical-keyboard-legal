Delete Mechanical Keyboard Data

For RGB Mechanical Keyboard Sounds. Effective 9 October 2026. There is no separate Mechanical Keyboard account. Local deletion, purchase restoration and service-held records are different operations.

Delete local data

Open the companion app, choose Settings → Clear my data, read the explanation and confirm. This removes settings, saved designs, imported photo copies, personal words, saved clipboard entries and local progress. Cached purchase ownership is cleared and can be restored separately. If stored data cannot be read safely, the recovery screen offers Retry and an explicit Clear my data action; the app does not silently reset corrupted data.

Delete selected items

Delete a design from Library, remove individual personal words or clipboard entries from Settings, or choose Remove photo. These actions do not delete original gallery photos. Unpinned clipboard entries become unavailable after one hour and are removed on a subsequent state update; pinned items need manual removal.

Diagnostics

This version includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, so there is no diagnostics data to delete.

Uninstalling

Android removes app-local files when uninstalling. It does not recall scene codes already shared, remove email messages or delete store purchase history. Switch to another enabled input method as needed.

Request deletion of support or service data

Email contact@zoopet.in. State that your request concerns Mechanical Keyboard and identify the support conversation or service involved. We will assess the request under applicable law and explain any necessary retention, inability to identify a record or referral to the relevant provider. Do not send typed passwords, full purchase tokens or unnecessary identity documents.

Purchases and limits

Clearing local data does not cancel, refund or delete a store transaction. Use the store's own purchase/refund process. The app sends no purchase receipts to our servers; the issuing store keeps its own purchase records. Data necessary for fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal duties may be retained where permitted and explained to you.

See the Privacy Policy for the complete data scope.
